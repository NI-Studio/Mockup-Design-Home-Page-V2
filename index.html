A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Questions About eStamp?

We have answered the most frequently asked questions to help you get started with confidence.

What is eStamp?

eStamp is an independent digital platform that simplifies stamp duty calculations, document submission, and online payment so you can receive your stamp slip easily.

Is eStamp officially recognised by LHDN?

eStamp is a privately operated technology platform that facilitates user access to the stamp duty process through integration or links with authorised systems. Stamp certificates remain under the jurisdiction of LHDN.

How long does the stamping process take?

Processing time depends on document completeness and verification, with real-time status updates shown in your account dashboard.

Do I need to visit any office?

No. The entire process from uploading documents and paying online to receiving your stamp slip is completed digitally.

What types of documents can be stamped?

You can stamp Tenancy Agreements, Service Agreements, Loan Agreements, Bonds, Employment Contracts, Statutory Declarations, NDAs, Letters of Undertaking, and General Agreements.

What file format do I need to upload?

Documents are typically uploaded in clear PDF format so all agreement details and signatures can be verified accurately.

Where is my stamp slip stored?

Once stamped, your slip is sent directly to your email and stored securely in your eStamp account dashboard for access anytime.

How much does it cost to use eStamp?

Fixed Duty plans start at RM30 (RM20 processing fee + RM10 stamp duty), while Ad Valorem Duty plans have a RM50 processing fee plus stamp duty based on document value.

What payment methods are accepted?

Online payments are accepted securely via supported Malaysian online banking and digital payment methods during checkout.

Is the stamp duty amount calculated automatically?

Yes. eStamp automatically calculates stamp duty based on current LHDN rates before you proceed with payment.

Is the confidentiality of my documents guaranteed?

Yes. Your documents are stored and managed securely in compliance with applicable data privacy standards and recorded with audit-ready timestamps.

Stamp Duty Rate Reference

1. Sale / Transfer of Property, Assets or Business

Price RangeRateCalculation Formula
First RM100,0001%Price × 1%
RM100,001 to RM500,0002%(Price - RM100,000) × 2%
RM500,001 to RM1,000,0003%(Price - RM500,000) × 3%
Above RM1,000,0004%(Price - RM1,000,000) × 4%
Example: Property RM500,000 = (RM100,000 × 1%) + (RM400,000 × 2%) = RM1,000 + RM8,000 = RM9,000

2. Loan Agreement

TypeRateFormula
SME / Shariah-Compliant / Conventional Loan0.5%Loan Amount × 0.5%
Example: Loan RM400,000 = RM400,000 × 0.5% = RM2,000

3. Service Agreement

TypeRateFormula
Service Agreement0.1%Contract Value × 0.1%
Example: Contract RM100,000 = RM100,000 × 0.1% = RM100

4. Tenancy / Lease Agreement

Tenancy PeriodRateFormula
Lease period ≤ 1 yearRM1⌈Annual Rent ÷ 250⌉ × RM1
Lease period ≤ 3 yearsRM3⌈Annual Rent ÷ 250⌉ × RM3
Lease period ≤ 5 yearsRM5⌈Annual Rent ÷ 250⌉ × RM5
Lease period > 5 yearsRM7⌈Annual Rent ÷ 250⌉ × RM7
Example: Rent RM2,000/month × 12 = RM24,000/year. ⌈RM24,000 ÷ 250⌉ = 96 units. Period 1 to 3 years = 96 × RM3 = RM288
Note: Charged for every RM250 of annual rent or part thereof. Formula uses ceiling function.
